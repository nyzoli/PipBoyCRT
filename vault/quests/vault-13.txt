# Vault 13: The Water Chip Requisition

[1]
The Overseer's memo blinks on your terminal at 06:00 sharp. The water
recycler has developed what Maintenance calls "a character flaw" and what
everyone else calls a flood. You are to requisition a replacement chip.
Form 13-B is attached. All nine pages of it.

You pull on your jumpsuit. The corridor smells of ozone and yesterday's
noodles.

-> 2 Report to the Overseer first, as procedure demands
-> 3 Go straight to the Maintenance level and see the damage yourself

[2]
The Overseer's office is exactly as warm as his welcome. He explains, at
length, that the vault has survived two hundred years on procedure, and
that procedure begins with the correct form. He stamps Form 13-B twice,
then stamps the stamp.

You leave with an authorisation slip and a faint headache.

Record the Authorisation Slip on your Action Chart.

-> 3 Take the lift down to Maintenance
-> 4 Detour past the storeroom while the slip is still warm

[3]
Maintenance level is ankle deep and rising. A supervisor in waders points
at a hatch marked DO NOT ENTER, then at you, then back at the hatch, in
the universal gesture for promotion.

Beyond the hatch something skitters.

-> 5 Open the hatch
-> 4 Visit the storeroom for a proper tool first

[4]
The storeroom clerk has been asleep since the Great Inventory. He wakes
long enough to slide a crate across the counter and go back to sleep.
Inside: one Pipe Wrench, one tin of Iguana-on-a-Stick, and a laminated
card reading TOOL RETURN IS MANDATORY.

Record the Pipe Wrench and one Meal on your Action Chart.

-> 5 Head for the hatch

[5]
The hatch opens onto the coolant crawlspace. Your lamp finds two eyes,
then the rest of the rad roach attached to them. It is the size of a
footstool and twice as confident.

!combat Rad Roach 9 12

If you would rather not test the wrench, you may evade into the crawlspace
and keep going.

-> 6 If you win the fight, or evade, crawl on
-> 8 If you retreat to the corridor instead

[6]
Past the crawlspace is the spare parts locker, sealed since 2161. The
keypad wants four digits. Above it someone has helpfully scratched
"THE YEAR WE ALL AGREED NOT TO TALK ABOUT".

Pick a number from the Random Number Table.

-> 7 If you pick 0-4, the panel accepts it
-> 8 If you pick 5-9, the panel does not

[7]
The locker holds a water chip in its original packaging, complete with a
cheerful sticker: VAULT-TEC — TOMORROW, TODAY, PROBABLY.

Record the Water Chip as a Special Item.

-> 9 Carry it back up to Maintenance

[8]
You climb back to the corridor with nothing but wet boots. The supervisor
looks at you the way supervisors do. There is, he admits, a second locker.
There is always a second locker.

-> 6 Try the keypad again

[9]
The recycler swallows the chip and hums a note it has not managed in
decades. The water level drops. Somewhere above you, the Overseer is
already drafting a memo about the correct procedure for gratitude.

Vault 13 is dry. You have earned your noodles.

THE END
