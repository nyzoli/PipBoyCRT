# Vault 13: The Water Chip Requisition

[1]
The Overseer's memo blinks on your terminal at 06:00 sharp. The water
recycler has developed what Maintenance calls "a character flaw" and what
everyone else calls a flood. You are to requisition a replacement chip.
Form 13-B is attached. All nine pages of it.

You pull on your jumpsuit. The corridor smells of ozone and yesterday's
noodles.

-> 2 Report to the Overseer first, as procedure demands
-> 3 Go straight down and see the damage yourself

[2]
The Overseer's office is exactly as warm as his welcome. He explains, at
length, that the vault has survived two hundred years on procedure, and
that procedure begins with the correct form. He stamps Form 13-B twice,
then stamps the stamp.

You leave with an Authorisation Slip and a faint headache. Write the
Authorisation Slip on your Action Chart now; nobody will do it for you.

-> 4 Stop at the storeroom while the slip is still warm
-> 5 Take the lift straight down to Maintenance

[3]
You skip the office. Somewhere behind you a form goes unstamped, and the
vault's paperwork shifts uneasily in its sleep.

The lift smells of wet carpet, which is impressive, as there is no carpet.

-> 5 Step out on the Maintenance level
-> 6 Stop one floor early at the tool cupboard

[4]
The storeroom clerk has been asleep since the Great Inventory. He wakes
just long enough to slide a crate over the counter and go back under.
Inside: one Pipe Wrench, one tin of Iguana-on-a-Stick, and a laminated
card reading TOOL RETURN IS MANDATORY.

Add the Pipe Wrench to your Weapons and one Meal to your chart.

-> 5 Down to Maintenance

[5]
Maintenance is ankle deep and rising. A supervisor in waders points at a
hatch marked DO NOT ENTER, then at you, then back at the hatch, in the
universal gesture for promotion.

Beyond the hatch something skitters.

-> 7 Open the hatch and crawl in
-> 6 Find a proper tool first

[6]
The tool cupboard contains a crowbar, a bucket of bolts labelled BOLTS
(ASSORTED, MOSTLY), and a poster of a smiling family under the slogan
VAULT-TEC: YOUR TOMORROW IS OUR YESTERDAY.

Add the Crowbar to your Weapons if you want it. It is, at minimum, a
very honest piece of metal.

-> 5 Back to the flooded corridor
-> 7 Straight on to the hatch

[7]
The hatch opens onto the coolant crawlspace. Your lamp finds two eyes,
then the rest of the rad roach attached to them. It is the size of a
footstool and roughly twice as confident.

!combat Rad Roach 9 12

The crawlspace runs on past it, so you may evade instead of fighting to
the end.

-> 8 Crawl on to the spare parts locker
-> 9 Back out to the corridor

[8]
The spare parts locker has been sealed since 2161. The keypad wants four
digits. Above it someone has scratched THE YEAR WE ALL AGREED NOT TO
TALK ABOUT, which is helpful if you were there and useless if you were
not.

Pick a number from the Random Number Table. If it is 0–4, turn to 10;
if it is 5–9, turn to 11.

-> 10 If it is 0–4, turn to 10
-> 11 If it is 5–9, turn to 11

[9]
You reverse out with nothing but wet boots. The supervisor looks at you
the way supervisors do, then admits there is a second locker. There is
always a second locker.

-> 8 Try the keypad again
-> 12 Go up and report the difficulty through channels

[10]
The door sighs open. Inside, in its original packaging, sits a water
chip, complete with a cheerful sticker: VAULT-TEC - TOMORROW, TODAY,
PROBABLY.

Record the Water Chip under Special Items. The Pip-Boy will not do it
for you, and the recycler will ask.

-> 13 Carry it back up

[11]
The keypad buzzes, thinks about it, and buzzes again. Somewhere three
levels up, a light comes on in a room nobody has entered since the
lights were installed.

-> 8 Try once more
-> 12 Leave it and go through channels

[12]
Back in the office, the Overseer listens with the expression of a man
who has read every form ever printed and enjoyed most of them. The
problem, he says, is that you are on Form 13-B, and a failed locker
requires Form 13-C.

He stamps something. It is not clear what.

-> 14 Take 13-C to the requisition desk
-> 9 Go back down and have another go at the locker

[13]
You climb with the chip under one arm. The lift is dark, the call button
is warm, and a small handwritten sign reads OUT OF ORDER - USE THE
OTHER ONE. There is no other one.

-> 15 Take the stairwell
-> 16 Take the service shaft

[14]
The requisition desk is staffed by a clerk who has perfected the art of
looking busy at a desk with nothing on it. He asks for your
authorisation.

If you have the Authorisation Slip on your Action Chart, turn to 17. If
you do not, turn to 18.

-> 17 If you have the Authorisation Slip
-> 18 If you do not have it

[15]
The stairwell is loud with dripping. Halfway down, something large and
unimpressed by your jumpsuit comes up the other way.

!combat Mole Rat 11 16

-> 19 Push past and carry on up
-> 20 Give ground and go down instead

[16]
The service shaft is a ladder in a pipe, which is a fine thing to be
until you are on it. The rungs are slick.

Pick a number from the Random Number Table. If it is 0-4, turn to 21;
if it is 5-9, turn to 19.

-> 21 If it is 0-4, turn to 21
-> 19 If it is 5-9, turn to 19

[17]
The clerk reads the slip twice, holds it to the light, and stamps it
with obvious pleasure. In return you receive a Spare Chip Voucher, which
he explains is worth exactly one spare chip and nothing else in this or
any other world.

Record the Spare Chip Voucher under Special Items.

-> 22 Take it to the storeroom

[18]
Without the slip you are, in the clerk's professional opinion, a person
with an interesting story and no paperwork. He is sympathetic. He is
also immovable.

-> 2 Go and get the slip stamped properly
-> 23 Try the corridor of vending machines instead

[19]
Level 2 smells almost dry. The corridor splits: hydroponics one way,
the reactor the other, and a maintenance arrow pointing firmly at the
floor.

-> 24 Go through hydroponics
-> 25 Take the reactor corridor

[20]
Down is darker and wetter. The sub-basement has decided to become a
water feature and is making a good job of it.

-> 26 Wade to the pump room
-> 15 Climb back up the stairwell

[21]
Your boot finds the one rung that has been waiting two hundred years
for a boot. You arrive in the sub-basement faster than planned and
considerably wetter.

Lose 2 ENDURANCE if you are keeping score honestly, and you should be.

-> 26 Pick yourself up and head for the pump room

[22]
The storeroom clerk surfaces, inspects the voucher, and produces a chip
from a shelf that was empty the last time anyone looked at it. He does
not explain. Storeroom clerks never do.

Record the Water Chip under Special Items.

-> 13 Carry it up

[23]
The vending corridor hums with six machines, five of them dark. The
survivor is a Nuka machine with a hand-lettered sign: EXACT CAP ONLY -
NO REFUNDS - NO DISCUSSION.

-> 28 Put a cap in
-> 24 Walk on to hydroponics

[24]
Hydroponics is warm, green, and smells like the idea of outdoors. Old
Hilda is up to her elbows in a tuber tray and does not look up.

"You're the chip one," she says. "Everyone's the chip one this week."

-> 29 Ask her about the recycler
-> 25 Leave her to the tubers

[25]
The reactor corridor is lit the colour of a headache. The nest under the
conduit is not a nest so much as a committee, and it has noticed you.

!combat Rad Roach Swarm 13 20

-> 30 Clear the corridor and go on
-> 26 Retreat to the sub-basement

[26]
The pump room is knee deep and patient. Somewhere below the surface a
pump is doing its best, which is not much, and doing it loudly.

-> 31 Work the valve wheel
-> 20 Back to the stairwell

[27]
The storeroom, again. The clerk, again. He slides a second crate over
the counter without opening his eyes. Inside is a chip, a spanner, and
a note that says I KNEW YOU'D BE BACK.

-> 13 Take it up
-> 32 Stop by the Overseer's lounge on the way

[28]
The machine takes your cap with a sound of enormous satisfaction.

Pick a number from the Random Number Table. If it is 0, 1 or 2, turn to
33; if it is 3 to 9, turn to 29.

-> 33 If it is 0, 1 or 2, turn to 33
-> 29 If it is 3 to 9, turn to 29

[29]
Hilda wipes her hands and tells you what everyone above Level 2 has
forgotten: the recycler does not want a new chip so much as an honest
one. The last three were requisitioned, approved, stamped, and wrong.

She gives you a Tuber. Add it to your Backpack. It is not much of a
weapon, but it is a very good tuber.

-> 24 Back to the corridor
-> 34 Go down to the recycler chamber

[30]
The corridor is quiet in the way corridors are when the committee has
adjourned. Behind the conduit, a service hatch leads down towards the
recycler.

-> 34 Take the hatch down
-> 31 Detour through the pump room

[31]
The valve wheel turns with a groan you feel in your teeth. The water
drops four inches and stops, as if to see whether you are impressed.

-> 34 On to the recycler chamber
-> 26 Stay and wrestle the pump a while longer

[32]
The Overseer's lounge is dim and smells of pipe smoke that predates the
pipe. He is running a slideshow titled PROCEDURE: A CELEBRATION, and he
is on slide eleven of two hundred.

-> 35 Sit down for one slide. Just one.
-> 12 Make your excuses and go back to the office

[33]
The machine returns your cap, then returns it again, then keeps
returning it. The panel slides open and a vending unit unfolds itself to
its full and frankly unnecessary height.

!combat Vend-Tron 8 10

-> 29 Step over the parts and go to hydroponics
-> 23 Back into the corridor and pretend that did not happen

[34]
The recycler chamber is all pipes and apology. The intake tray sits open
at chest height, exactly the size of a water chip, waiting with the
patience of a machine that has been let down before.

If you have the Water Chip on your Action Chart, turn to 36. If you do
not, turn to 37.

-> 36 If you have the Water Chip
-> 37 If you do not have it

[35]
Slide eleven becomes slide twelve. Somewhere around slide forty the
Overseer says, without turning round, that a person who sits through
this is a person who understands paperwork, and that the vault will
need a Deputy Overseer of Requisitions.

-> 40 Accept the position
-> 34 Excuse yourself and take the chip down

[36]
The tray accepts the chip with a click that sounds, if you are honest,
slightly surprised.

Pick a number from the Random Number Table. If it is 0–5, turn to 38;
if it is 6–9, turn to 39.

-> 38 If it is 0–5, turn to 38
-> 39 If it is 6–9, turn to 39

[37]
The tray is open, the chamber is waiting, and your hands are empty. The
recycler makes a noise that in a person would be a sigh.

-> 8 Go back to the spare parts locker
-> 39 Go up and tell them it cannot be done

[38]
The recycler swallows the chip and hums a note it has not managed in
decades. The water level drops. A pump somewhere finds its rhythm and
keeps it.

Vault 13 is dry. Somewhere above you the Overseer is already drafting a
memo about the correct procedure for gratitude, and the storeroom clerk
is asleep, and hydroponics smells like the idea of outdoors, and you
have earned your noodles.

THE END

[39]
The chamber floods with a politeness that makes it worse. By the time
the bulkheads close, the water is over the intake tray, over the
walkway, and rising with the calm of something that has read the
schedule and is running early.

The report will say the requisition was correctly filed. That is the
part they always get right.

THE END

[40]
You accept. There is a badge. There is a desk. There is, within a week,
a Form 13-D of your own devising, and by the end of the year nobody
below Level 3 may open a cupboard without it.

The water is never fixed. But the paperwork describing the water is the
finest in the vault system, and twice a year a clerk from Vault 8 comes
to admire it.

THE END
